# India Connectivity Intelligence 2026

## Executive summary

India's connectivity market continued to grow through 2026, while the difference between urban and rural internet subscription density remains material. The evidence indicates that Jio's opportunity is to keep scaling 5G, fixed broadband, and AirFiber while treating satellite connectivity as a potential complement for hard-to-reach locations.

## Headline findings

| Indicator | Latest observation | Why it matters |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Internet subscriptions | 1,114.17 million, June 2026 | Increased 1.96% from March 2026. |
| Broadband subscriptions | 1,087.50 million, June 2026 | Increased 2.03% from March 2026. |
| Internet subscription density | Urban 129.03; rural 49.07 per 100 people | Density counts subscriptions, not unique people, but shows a persistent access gap. |
| 5G FWA subscriptions | 13.45 million, August 2026 | 6.98 million were reported in rural areas. |
| Jio customer base | 533.3 million, June 2026 | Company-reported base; includes about 285 million True5G users. |
| JioAirFiber homes | 14.0 million, June 2026 | Company-reported home-connection count. |

## Interpretation

Terrestrial mobile, fixed, and fixed-wireless services are the core of the market today. Satellite connectivity may expand options where terrestrial coverage or backhaul is difficult, but government authorisation milestones do not demonstrate retail availability, price, uptake, or market share.

## Scope and limitations

This public-source edition uses publications from TRAI, DoT, PIB, and Reliance Industries, with an evidence cut-off of 14 October 2026. It contains no customer survey, customer-level data, synthetic data, or third-party market estimates. It therefore does not claim measured satisfaction, willingness to pay, churn prediction, or customer lifetime value.
