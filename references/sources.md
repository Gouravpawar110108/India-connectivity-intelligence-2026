# Source register

Evidence cut-off: 14 October 2026. All sources below are official primary sources. Retrieved on 14 October 2026 unless otherwise noted.

## S1 - TRAI quarterly performance indicators, April-June 2026

- Publisher: Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI)
- Published: 12 October 2026
- Data period: quarter ended 30 June 2026
- Key uses: internet and broadband subscriber counts; subscriber density per 100 population; wireless-service ARPU.
- Notes: the report defines broadband using a minimum download capability of 2 Mbps. Subscriber density is a subscription count per population, not unique-person adoption.
- Source: https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/QPIR_12102026.pdf

- Key uses: annual internet and broadband growth; financial-year wireless ARPU; internet subscriber density.
- Source: https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-10/YIR_05102026_0.pdf

## S3 - TRAI telecom subscription data, August 2026

- Publisher: TRAI
- Published: 28 September 2026
- Data period: subscriptions at end of August 2026
- Key uses: broadband provider counts and share; wired, mobile, and fixed-wireless components; 5G FWA rural/urban split.
- Notes: the release reports broadband with and without M2M; the value and denominator must be named. Jio's 538.03 million broadband count includes mobile broadband and is not a home-broadband count.
- Source: https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/PR_No125of2026_0.pdf
- Report listing: https://www.trai.gov.in/release-publication/reports/telecom-subscriptions-reports

## Retrieval and calculation notes

- TRAI and RIL values were extracted from their linked reports. The PDF charts and market calculations use these report values and the source definitions above.
- Growth formula: (current-period value - prior-period value) / prior-period value x 100.
- Urban-rural density gap: urban subscriptions per 100 population minus rural subscriptions per 100 population.
- Rural 5G FWA share: rural 5G FWA subscriptions divided by all 5G FWA subscriptions.
- No survey, customer-level data, synthetic customer records, or third-party estimates are included.
